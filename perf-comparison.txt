Analysis
--------
Time: jp_alloc v2 is the fastest at 53.6s avg — 2.4% faster than the mempool
ref and 9.6% faster than system tup 0.8. The lock-free CAS freelists and
headerless sized allocations give a consistent edge. Optimizations applied in
v2 (pool floor 32->16, jp_realloc_sized same-page fix, mremap for large
reallocs) are edge-case improvements that don't significantly affect parse
time since parse rarely does large reallocs.

RSS: jp_alloc v2 uses 372 MB vs 332 MB for mempool (+12%) and 313 MB for
system tup (+19%). The increase is from power-of-2 rounding waste (up to 60%
for struct tent_tree). The old mempool packed objects at exact sizeof with
zero waste.

Trade-off: ~2-10% faster parse for ~12% more transient memory (freed at exit).
The memory overhead is inherent to the buddy-allocator design and only matters
if peak RSS is a constraint.
